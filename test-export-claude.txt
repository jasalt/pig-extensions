  Findings worth knowing

  - PiG bug: the host's shell-execution timeout kills the shell but not its
    background children. The call then blocks until they exit (12 s for a 1.5 s
    timeout). Scheduled shell jobs therefore run in their own process group, and
    a timeout or shutdown kills the whole tree.
